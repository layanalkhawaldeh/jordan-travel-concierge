Jordan Entry Visa Requirements and Jordan Pass Guide
Last Updated: August 2026

1. General Entry Visa:
- Most foreign tourists visiting Jordan require an entry visa.
- A standard single-entry tourist visa can be purchased directly upon arrival at Queen Alia International Airport (Amman) or Aqaba land border.
- The cost of the single-entry visa is exactly 40 JOD (approximately $56 USD). Payment must be made in Jordanian Dinars (JOD). Exchange booths and ATMs are available at the airport arrivals terminal before immigration.

2. The Jordan Pass - Highly Recommended:
- The Jordan Pass is an official initiative by the Ministry of Tourism.
- It includes prepaid entry fees to over 40 tourist sites in Jordan, including Petra, Jerash, Wadi Rum, and Amman Citadel.
- Crucial Benefit: The tourist entry visa fee of 40 JOD is completely WAIVED if you purchase the Jordan Pass before arriving in Jordan, provided that you stay in Jordan for a minimum of 3 consecutive nights (4 days).
- Jordan Pass Packages:
  * Jordan Wanderer (1-day Petra visit): 70 JOD (approx. $99 USD)
  * Jordan Explorer (2-day Petra visit): 75 JOD (approx. $106 USD)
  * Jordan Expert (3-day Petra visit): 80 JOD (approx. $113 USD)
- Buying a Jordan Pass before you travel is highly recommended because a single-day entry ticket to Petra alone costs 50 JOD. Thus, the pass saves you money instantly if you visit Petra and stay for at least 3 nights.

3. Aqaba Special Economic Zone (ASEZA) Visa Waiver:
- Tourists arriving directly at Aqaba (via King Hussein International Airport or land/sea borders) are eligible for a free visa, under ASEZA regulations. To maintain this free status, you are expected to register with ASEZA and leave the country within 30 days.

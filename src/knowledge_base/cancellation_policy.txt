Cancellation Policy & Refund Guidelines - Al-Nashama Travel Agency
Last Updated: August 2026

We at Al-Nashama Travel Agency understand that plans can change. Below is our official policy for trip cancellations and booking modifications:

1. Standard Cancellation Refund Windows:
- Cancellations made 14 days or more before the scheduled trip start date: 100% full refund of the total booking cost.
- Cancellations made between 7 and 14 days (inclusive) before the scheduled trip start date: 50% refund of the total booking cost.
- Cancellations made less than 7 days before the scheduled trip start date: 0% refund (no refund will be issued).

2. Non-Refundable Custom Bookings:
- Certain high-demand bookings (including specialized desert luxury camps in Wadi Rum and hot air balloon rides) are non-refundable once confirmed, regardless of when the cancellation occurs. This is because these providers charge us 100% upfront deposits.

3. Modification Policy:
- Clients can request to change travel dates up to 5 days before departure without any agency modification fees, subject to hotel and driver availability. If hotels charge a price difference for the new dates, the client must pay the difference.

4. Force Majeure & Flight Cancellations:
- If flights are cancelled by the airline or travel is disrupted due to force majeure, Al-Nashama will issue a credit voucher valid for 2 years towards any future trip to Jordan, rather than cash refunds.
